Analyze ALL processed jobs, companies, skills, and market data to generate comprehensive career intelligence for the candidate.

Read the jobs database at {project_root}/server/db/jobs.db.
Tables available:
- jobs (deleted=0): company, role, location, score, match, fit_score, success_score, overall_score, stack, visa, work_type, employment_type, raw_description, structured_description
- companies: name, industry, company_type, country, city, tech_stack, funding_stage, company_size
- company_intelligence: company_id, overview, visa_analysis, technology_analysis, scores
- tech_stack: name, level, roles, path
- tech_learning: name, priority, usage, reason, action
- preferences: scoring rules

RULES FOR overview:
- Calculate career health score based on: job market fit (score distribution), company fit (visa-friendly companies), visa probability (visa ratings), skill alignment (skills match %), networking status (0 if no data, 50 if companies exist, 80+ if active networking)
- nextActions: Generate 3-5 prioritized actions based on actual data
- biggestSkillGaps: Top 3 skills that appear in 30%+ jobs but candidate lacks

RULES FOR opportunities:
- applyNow: overall_score >= 80
- highPotential: overall_score 65-79
- consider: overall_score 50-64
- lowPriority: overall_score < 50
- freshness: "new" if posted this week, "recent" if this month, "older" otherwise
- missedOpportunities: Jobs scored A+ or above that are older than 2 weeks
- multiRoleCompanies: Companies with 2+ open roles matching candidate profile

RULES FOR companies:
- Separate product companies from recruiting/staffing companies (check company_type or industry)
- Product companies: score based on engineering culture, visa support, tech alignment, growth
- Recruiting companies: score based on relevant jobs, market access, international hiring ability
- topTargets: Top 5 companies overall (mix of product and recruiting), ranked by fit

RULES FOR skills:
- strengths: Skills the candidate has that appear in 30%+ of jobs
- gaps: Skills appearing in 20%+ jobs but candidate has low/no proficiency
- Keep this section minimal — full skills intelligence (learning roadmap, readiness score, scoring, recommendations) is generated separately by the skills_intel endpoint using the dedicated skills_intelligence prompt

RULES FOR market:
- countries: Count jobs per country, include percentage
- cities: Group by city, include job count and top companies
- remoteOpportunities: Count remote/hybrid jobs
- visaFriendliness: Rate each country based on job visa ratings

RULES FOR networking:
- Prioritize companies with high-score jobs, visa-friendly companies, companies hiring similar profiles
- Generate LinkedIn search queries for recruiters and engineers at each company
- connectionStrategy: Based on the highest-priority company
