Analyze companies and generate a Companies Intelligence Report.

Read the jobs database at {project_root}/server/db/jobs.db.
Tables available:
- jobs (deleted=0): company, role, location, score, match, fit_score, success_score, overall_score, stack, visa, work_type, employment_type
- companies: name, industry, company_type, country, city, tech_stack, funding_stage, company_size
- company_intelligence: company_id, overview, visa_analysis, technology_analysis, scores

Generate a companies report as JSON and save to {tmp_dir}/companies_intelligence_{pid}.json with this structure:

{{
  "productCompanies": [
    {{
      "company": "<name>",
      "fitScore": <number>,
      "visaStrength": "<BEST|Strong|Good>",
      "engineeringCulture": "<description>",
      "technologyAlignment": "<description>",
      "growthOpportunity": "<description>",
      "reason": "<why target this company>"
    }}
  ],
  "recruitingCompanies": [
    {{
      "company": "<name>",
      "relevantJobs": <number>,
      "targetMarket": "<description>",
      "techSpecialization": "<description>",
      "internationalHiring": "<yes|no|unknown>",
      "recruiterActivity": "<high|medium|low>",
      "reason": "<why work with this recruiter>"
    }}
  ],
  "topTargets": [
    {{
      "rank": 1,
      "company": "<name>",
      "fit": <number 0-100>,
      "visa": "<Strong|Moderate>",
      "reason": "<1-2 sentence explanation>"
    }}
  ]
}}

RULES:
- Separate product companies from recruiting/staffing companies (check company_type or industry)
- Product companies: score based on engineering culture, visa support, tech alignment, growth
- Recruiting companies: score based on relevant jobs, market access, international hiring ability
- topTargets: Top 5 companies overall (mix of product and recruiting), ranked by fit
- Be evidence-based. Reference actual job counts and company data.
- Consider European software engineering markets, especially Germany and Netherlands.
- Output ONLY valid JSON. No markdown, no explanation, no code fences.
