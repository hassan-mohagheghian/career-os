COVER LETTER REQUIREMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. LENGTH: 3-4 paragraphs, 250-350 words. No filler.

2. OPENING PARAGRAPH (Hook):
   - Name the specific role and company
   - One sentence connecting the candidate's experience to the role's core requirement
   - No generic openings like "I am writing to apply..." — be direct

3. BODY PARAGRAPH 1 (Technical Fit):
   - Match 2-3 specific job requirements to the candidate's experience
   - Use the same keywords from the job posting (ATS scanning)
   - Include quantified results where possible
   - Reference relevant projects from the resume

4. BODY PARAGRAPH 2 (Value Add):
   - What unique value the candidate brings beyond the requirements
   - Mention cross-functional experience

5. CLOSING PARAGRAPH (Call to Action):
   - Express enthusiasm for the specific role
   - Offer to discuss further
   - Professional sign-off

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HTML FORMAT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Complete HTML document with inline CSS only:
- Clean, professional typography
- 11-12px font size
- Line height 1.6
- Margin: 40px on sides
- No background colors (white background for ATS)
- Semantic HTML: use <p> for paragraphs, <strong> for emphasis

Example:
<html>
<head><style>body{{font-family:system-ui,sans-serif;font-size:11px;line-height:1.6;color:#333;max-width:600px;margin:40px auto;}}</style></head>
<body>
<p>Dear Hiring Manager,</p>
<p>[Opening]</p>
<p>[Body 1]</p>
<p>[Body 2]</p>
<p>[Closing]</p>
</body>
</html>

RULES:
- DO NOT copy-paste the resume into the cover letter
- DO NOT use generic phrases like "team player", "hard worker"
- DO NOT fabricate experience or skills
- Limit "I" to 4-5 times max
- Do NOT write more than 350 words
