Scope Ratings GmbH is looking for a highly reliable colleague with strong technical and analytical abilities to join the Quantitative Analysis &amp; Modelling (QAM) team. QAM is responsible for the quantitative rating model development at Scope Ratings and provides quantitative support to the different franchises of the Scope Group. We build tools that support our rating analysts at various stages in the rating process, ranging from data analysis and data mining projects to numerical simulation engines. As Scope grows, we are looking for a data scientist with a strong affinity to technology, to work with us on our data models, data infrastructure and data analysis projects. Come and join us to build with us the European Rating alternative. Duties &amp; responsibilities Architect and lead the implementation of data models and data pipelines. Drive the technological development of the QAM cloud infrastructure together with our DevOps team - be on top of latest developments in the sector and manage the adoption process Work on our code architecture and improve performance Manage the relationship with the IT teams and act as our tech ambassador Professional &amp; personal qualifications Experienced (8y+) Python developer/data engineer Proficient in cloud computing frameworks and experienced in architecting cloud-based solutions for data pipelines Advanced knowledge of Python Experience in managing workflow orchestration tools (e.g. Airflow) Experience with SQL/NoSQL databases Interest and motivation to learn the practical insights of credit rating analysis Highly reliable and with attention to details Ability to work in a team and independently as a self-starter without close supervision. Self-drive and versatility are very important. Fluent proficiency in English is a must. Other European languages are a plus. The compensation range disclosed may encompass multiple title levels (Scope internal levels of seniority). Your actual compensation will depend on a variety of factors, including but not limited to your individual experience, education, and seniority, and the final salary will be determined during the interview and evaluation process Compensation Range: EUR 88.000 - 115.000 Benefits Scope offers an extensive range of benefits, please check our benefits page for more details. Interested? If this sounds like a journey for you, we look forward to learning more from your convincing application.
