Direct message the job poster from WorkGenius Group Shailee Gohel Shailee Gohel Senior Tech Recruiter bei WorkGenius Berlin GmbH | Matchmaker | People and Tech Enthusiast Employment Type: Full-time | Permanent Role: Hybrid | 2-3 days per week to Hamburg office Open Positions Number of Positions: 1 Software Engineer Back-end The role is open for a full-time, permanent employment and is intended to strengthen the back-end engineering capability across the team at both junior and senior levels. We’re Looking for a Certain Kind of Mind We need someone who doesn’t just write backend code, but understands that code lives in a larger ecosystem. Our product is a web application platform, and every decision you make will ripple across the entire system. We’re not interested in a checklist of technologies. We’re looking for exceptional engineering judgment. Who You Are You think in systems, not just endpoints. You understand that database choices affect frontend performance, API design shapes user experience, and infrastructure decisions impact product velocity. You’re rigorous. You enjoy deep problem-solving and aim for elegant, maintainable solutions. You don’t just fix bugs — you understand why they appeared. You’re intrinsically motivated. You care deeply about the quality of what you build. You think about edge cases because shipping something fragile is unacceptable. You get things done. When you say you will solve something, it gets solved. You balance speed with craftsmanship, and you know when to move fast and when to build for the long term. You have depth. Your experience goes beyond frameworks into the underlying principles of software development. You can explain why something works, not just how. Requirements / Skills You have a strong background as a Software Engineer or Backend Developer with deep experience in Python and modern backend frameworks such as FastAPI, Starlette, Pydantic, or Falcon . You are proficient in containerization and cloud-native tooling, including Docker , and optionally Kubernetes or AWS for deploying and operating services. You bring solid expertise in working with relational data stores through SQLAlchemy and Postgres , enabling robust, efficient data modeling and persistence in production systems. You have excellent English communication skills; the team operates fully in English. What You’ll Do Design, build, and own critical parts of our web platform, with a clear understanding of how each component fits into the whole user journey. Work closely with frontend and product teams to shape the entire stack because you understand the complete system. Make architectural decisions that consider performance, scalability, security, and developer experience — all at once. Be a cornerstone of technical excellence on the team. Where You’ll Work You’ll join a small, cross-functional team of nine. We operate on trust and autonomy: when you own a project, you own it end-to-end — from concept to outcome. You will have the freedom to choose your approach and the responsibility to see it through. Our team includes six developers (two backend-heavy), a designer, a QA, and a Product Owner. While most of us are based in Hamburg, we span across the USA and the Middle East, building a product that bridges distance through clear communication and shared purpose.
