Key Responsibilities Backend Architecture: Design and implement scalable Python services that form the backbone of our production and logistics infrastructure. Process Automation: Develop and optimize backend logic for inventory management, production scheduling, and demand forecasting to eliminate manual friction. System Evolution: Support the continuous improvement and integration of our Odoo ERP environment, ensuring data flows seamlessly across the entire business. Data Mastery: Deep knowledge of PostgreSQL or other relational databases, with the ability to optimize performance for complex, high-volume transactions.
