The Role In this role, you will help build and maintain the Python microservices that power the TerraLens platform, including services for searching, processing, and serving satellite imagery and geospatial data. This is not a generic CRUD backend role: you will work on systems that orchestrate complex processing workflows and AI inference pipelines, support human-in-the-loop analysis, and run reliably in demanding customer environments. You will build production Python services, design typed APIs, work with databases and service-to-service communication, improve observability and deployment reliability, and collaborate closely with frontend, geospatial, AI/ML, and SAR experts. Depending on your experience and interests, the role can grow into broader ownership of backend architecture, shared service libraries, processing infrastructure, deployment reliability, and technical leadership of backend platform development.
